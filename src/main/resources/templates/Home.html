INDEX 04 / PROTOCOL

RETRIEVAL STANDARD

Follow the 3-step security verification workflow to reclaim lost personal property at National University.

01

SUBMIT CLAIM

Provide specific identifying markers, serial numbers, password patterns, or purchase receipts via the secure web claim form.

02

ADMIN AUDIT

LSF administrators verify your description against the physically inspected item and generate a unique Pickup Verification Pass.

03

OFFICE PICKUP

Present your official Verification Pass and valid student/employee ID at the campus security desk to complete property release.
